# Citywide zoning coverage - research findings (current Seattle sources, read live 2026-10-09)

Sources: Municode Library CURRENT text of SMC Title 23 (Ord. 127376, 2025; 127375, 2025), read in the in-app browser; Seattle GIS `Current_Land_Use_Zoning_Detail_2` (325 distinct real designation rows saved as `tests/fixtures/seattle-zoning-designations.json`; layer attributes ZONING, BASE_ZONE, ZONELUT, CLASS_DESC, CATEGORY_DESC, MHA_VALUE, CHAPTER). Every figure below is the operative text; each rule candidate cites its sections and carries the interpretive caveats.

## Taxonomy
The layer's `ZONING` value is parsed (src/zoning/designation.ts) into family, zone code, MHA suffix (M/M1/M2), RC, pedestrian P, height suffix and MIO prefix, cross-checked against ZONELUT / BASE_ZONE / MHA presence. 325/325 real values parse; contradictions or unrecognized values are UNKNOWN (fail closed). "LR2 (0.75)" is an older incentive base-FAR suffix: kept as an unrecognized suffix, so claims that depend on MHA presence are not made for it. Governing chapters: NR 23.44; LR/MR/HR 23.45; RC 23.46 (+ underlying residential zone); NC/C 23.47A; SM 23.48; Downtown 23.49; Industrial 23.50/23.50A; MIO 23.69; MPC-YT 23.75.

## Split zoning - SMC 23.45.508.G
"the development standards of each zone shall be applied in that zone, and may not be used in any other zone, except that if both zones have the same development standards, the development standard shall be applied to the lot as a whole. If a lot or development site includes more than one zoning designation and a development standard is based on lot area, the lot area used ... shall be the portion of the contiguous area with the corresponding zoning designation."
Implementation: location claims use the zones under the proposed footprint (when it is placed on a lot with more than one designation); lot-wide claims use the lot's zones; a claim is answered only when every zone involved resolves it to the same single rule row; otherwise REQUIRES_VERIFICATION for that claim only. No majority zone is ever chosen.

## RC - SMC 23.46.002.B
"Developments that do not include commercial uses permitted according to this Chapter 23.46 are regulated according to the standards for the applicable residential zone." RC is therefore not treated as changing the residential accessory-structure standards.

## Multifamily (LR1-LR3, MR, HR) - SMC 23.45
- 23.45.518.H.1: "Detached garages, carports, or other accessory structures that are not accessory dwelling units are allowed in required rear or side setbacks, subject to: a. Any accessory structure located between a principal structure and a side lot line shall provide the setback required for the principal structure; b. Any portion of an accessory structure located more than 25 feet from a rear lot line shall be set back at least 5 feet from the side lot line; c. ... at least 7 feet from any lot line that abuts a street; and d. ... separated by at least 3 feet from all principal structures, including the eaves ..." Nothing allows a structure in the required FRONT setback (Table A: LR front 7 ft average / 5 ft minimum; rear 7/5 or 0 at an alley; side 5 ft; MR Table B: front and street side 7/5, rear 15 or 10 at an alley, interior side 7/5; HR structures of 85 ft or less follow the MR setbacks).
- 23.45.514.C: accessory structures other than ADUs in required setbacks or separations are limited to 12 ft (garage ridge +3 ft if pitched at least 4:12). Table A heights: LR1 32; LR2 and LR3 40 (50 for LR3 in regional/urban centers and SAODs), 32 without an MHA suffix. Table B: MR 80 (60 without MHA), HR 440.
- 23.45.519.A: 5 ft between structures containing floor area in LR and MR zones (not HR).
- 23.45.510: floor area ratio (LR1 1.3/1.0, LR2 1.4/1.1, LR3 1.8 or 2.3 / 1.2, MR 4.5/3.2, HR 7 base). There is NO lot-coverage limit anywhere in Chapter 23.45. 23.45.510.A: decks associated with a single dwelling unit are not gross floor area.
- 23.45.518.G.4, G.5, G.7: unenclosed decks up to 18 in; porches/steps; deck and balcony projections up to 4 ft if at least 5 ft from any lot line, no more than 20 ft wide.
- 23.45.518.H.7-H.8: fences in required setbacks up to 6 ft (4 ft in the front setback extended to side lot lines and in street-side setbacks extended to the front and rear lot lines; 8 ft / 6 ft absolute with slope averaging; arbors +2 ft; 4 ft on a bulkhead or wall; 9.5 ft combined on a new raising-grade wall); walls raising grade 6 ft; cut walls minimum necessary or 6 ft with fences 3 ft back. Identical figures to the NR provisions.
- 23.45.536: garage access from an improved alley; garage doors facing a street at least 18 ft from the street lot line (LR and MR); surface parking not within 20 ft of a street lot line.
- 23.45.522 (amenity area 20% of lot in LR), 23.45.524 (Green Factor 0.6 for more than one new unit; street trees), 23.45.529 (design standards: 3 ft path, entry with 3x3 weather protection, 20% windows within 40 ft of a street) - recorded for the ADU slice.

## Neighborhood Commercial and Commercial - SMC 23.47A
- 23.47A.014: no ground-level setback for a small structure except (B.1) a triangular corner setback where a lot abuts a residentially zoned lot's front/side corner, (B.2-B.3) upper-level setbacks above 13 ft along lot lines that abut or are across an alley from a residential zone, (B.5) no entrance, window or other opening within 5 ft of an abutting residential lot; G.1 decks with open railings may extend into a required setback but not within 5 ft of a residential lot unless accessory to residential use and no more than 18 in above grade; G.5 fences 6 ft (8 ft absolute), bulkheads/walls 6 ft, 9.5 ft combined, cut walls 3 ft; Chapter 23.53 setbacks may be added.
- 23.47A.012.A: height limit is the height mapped on the Official Land Use Map (the number in the designation; 30 ft is the lowest).
- 23.47A.013: floor area ratio 2.5 to 8.25 by mapped height; no lot-coverage limit.
- 23.47A.032: in NC zones parking may not be between a structure and a street lot line; alley access when improved; one curb cut otherwise.
- 23.47A.004 Table A: residential use is permitted outright in NC1-NC3 and C1 and is a CONDITIONAL USE in C2 (footnote 15). C2 is therefore not covered (an existing house there is a conditional/nonconforming question outside this screening).

## Garage separation from the principal structure, and the zone-aware expected-constraint audit (2026-10-09)

Read live on Municode (version Sep 25 2026 CURRENT; Ord. 127376) and cross-checked against the November 2025 print of SMC 23.45.518.

**Neighborhood Residential (Chapter 23.44).**
- 23.44.100.A: the minimum separation between structures containing floor area is 5 ft, except that where a driveway or parking aisle separates them it is 2 ft more than the required width of the driveway or aisle, or 24 ft, whichever is less. 23.44.100.C: eaves, gutters, roofs and weather protection may project up to 2 ft into a required separation.
- 23.44.090.I.2: enclosed structures that are not dwelling units are allowed in the rear setback if not within 5 ft of a rear lot line (non-alley), not more than 12 ft high and "separated from a dwelling unit by at least 3 feet, eave to eave" (the unenclosed-structure counterpart is H.8). 23.44.090.G.3: a garage may stand in the rear setback if not within 5 ft of the rear lot line.
- The earlier garage-specific rule (former 23.44.016.E.3: a detached garage in a required yard at least 5 ft from a principal structure "including eaves and gutters of all structures") and its terraced-garage exception (former 23.44.016.D.9.b) NO LONGER APPEAR in the chapter: a text search of all of 23.44.010-.190 finds no "terraced". The garage is therefore governed by 23.44.100.A and 23.44.090.I.2.c. An attached garage is part of the principal structure; the product screens detached garages only.
- A detached garage is a "structure containing floor area": a garage's floor area is not exempt from the floor area limits (23.44.050; for multifamily zones 23.45.510.D lists the exemptions and parking is not one).

**Lowrise and Midrise (Chapter 23.45).**
- 23.45.519 "Separations between structures": A (LR and MR zones only): 5 ft between structures containing floor area, with the same driveway/parking-aisle variation; B: eaves, gutters, etc. may project 2 ft; enclosed structures are not allowed in the required separation.
- 23.45.518.H.1 (the user's "J.1" - the lettering moved between the Nov 2025 print, where it is I.1, and the Sep 2026 text, where it is H.1): detached garages, carports or other accessory structures (not ADUs) are allowed in required rear or side setbacks if (a) not between a principal structure and a side lot line unless the principal structure's side setback is kept, (b) 5 ft from the side lot line beyond 25 ft of the rear lot line, (c) 7 ft from a street lot line, (d) "separated by at least 3 feet from all principal structures, including the eaves, gutters, and other projecting features of the principal structure". The Nov 2025 print also allowed accessory structures "in required separations"; the Sep 2026 text does not.
- Chapter 23.45 has no separate garage-to-principal-structure separation beyond 519.A and H.1.d. (519.A's 10-ft separation between principal structures, former 23.45.518.F, is no longer in the section.)

**Highrise.** 23.45.519.A applies in LR and MR zones only; the only separation is H.1.d (3 ft, for a garage standing in a required setback). No 5 ft rule.

**Neighborhood Commercial and Commercial (Chapter 23.47A).** No garage-to-principal-structure separation. The only separation provisions are a 30-ft separation between structures wider than 250 ft adjoining the same north-south street lot line, optional facade-modulation/building-separation breaks, and screening/street-level provisions. The claim is NOT_APPLICABLE; no rule row exists and the report lists nothing for it.

**Measurement basis and encoding.** The principal structure is Seattle's building outline (a roof edge; includes eaves); the garage is the footprint the customer placed. Thresholds: under 3 ft (minus the 2 ft mapping tolerance) fails whichever provision applies (the 5 ft is wall to wall and eaves may project 2 ft, so a roof-edge distance under about 3 ft cannot satisfy it); at or beyond 24 ft plus tolerance passes under every variant (driveway cap); between 5 ft plus tolerance and 24 ft plus tolerance it passes only when the customer states no driveway or parking aisle lies between the structures (the required width is not known); anything else is REQUIRES_VERIFICATION. In Highrise a short distance fails only when the garage is known to stand in a required setback. Rule type `GARAGE_SEPARATION`; rows `garage-nr-separation-2026`, `garage-mf-separation-lr-mr-2026`, `garage-mf-separation-hr-2026`.

**Audit of expected constraints across project types and zone families** (live pipeline on NR, LR1-3, MR, HR, NC2, NC2P, C1, a split lot): the "not yet screenable" notices that remained were (1) the garage separation in every family (fixed: rows for NR, LR/MR, HR; none for NC/C, where it does not exist), (2) the Highrise shed separation (fixed: Highrise row, 3 ft in a required setback, no 5 ft rule), (3) the Highrise garage access and driveway standards (fixed: SMC 23.45.536 applies chapter-wide; the 18 ft garage-door setback of 23.45.536.E is LR and MR only, so Highrise has its own row without it). Fence and deck reports listed no uncovered constraint in any family. ADU in MR, HR, NC and C remains a real implementation gap and is blocked at checkout with a plain reason; it is not a notice inside a paid report. Constraint expectations now come from `expectedConstraintTypesFor({ projectType, family, project })`: a constraint is listed only when the governing code for that zone family contains it.


## ADUs in Midrise, Highrise, Neighborhood Commercial (NC1-3) and Commercial 1 (2026-10-09)

**Source and provenance.** Seattle Municipal Code as published on Municode Library (https://library.municode.com/wa/seattle/codes/municipal_code), version "SEP 25, 2026 (CURRENT)", Ord. 127376 (2025), read live 2026-10-09 through the chapter nodes `TIT23LAUSCO_SUBTITLE_IIILAUSRE_CH23.42GEUSPR` (23.42.022), `..._CH23.45MU_23.45.514STHE` (Chapter 23.45) and `..._CH23.47ACO_23.47A.013FLARRA` (Chapter 23.47A). Each rule row carries `citation.smcSections`, the ordinance number and an `effectiveDateBasis` naming this version; the figures below are the values encoded.

**What 23.42.022 says (every zone where housing is allowed).** A: ADUs are a housing use allowed wherever housing uses are allowed. C: at most two ADUs per lot. D: attached, detached or stacked. E: "Unless otherwise provided in the standards of the underlying zone, accessory dwelling units shall be subject to the same standards as principal dwelling units." G.1: 1,000 sq ft (up to two bedrooms) or 1,200 sq ft (three or more); 1,500 sq ft only in an LR zone in a frequent transit service area on a lot not purchased for more than $1,000 in 20 years. H: conversions of accessory structures that existed before July 23, 2023 are allowed notwithstanding lot coverage and yard or setback provisions. I: no parking required. J: ADUs count in density. Chapter 23.45 mentions ADUs only to exclude them from the accessory-structure allowance in required setbacks (23.45.518.H.1) and from the 12 ft accessory height limit (23.45.514.C), so in MR and HR an ADU meets the same setback, height and floor-area standards as any dwelling.

**Midrise (MR) and Highrise (HR) - Chapter 23.45.** Setbacks 23.45.518.B Table B: front and street side 7 ft average / 5 ft minimum (none with an at-grade courtyard meeting Exhibit A); rear 15 ft, 10 ft where the rear lot line abuts an alley; interior side 7 average / 5 minimum for portions up to 42 ft and 10 / 7 above; 23.45.518.C: Highrise structures of 85 ft or less follow the MR table, taller ones another. Height 23.45.514.B Table B: MR 80 ft (60 ft without an MHA suffix), HR 440 ft; rooftop features +4 ft (I.2). Separation 23.45.519.A: 5 ft between structures containing floor area in LR and MR zones ONLY (none in HR). FAR 23.45.510: MR 4.5 (3.2 without MHA); HR base 7 (maximum 15 only through 23.45.516 and Chapter 23.58A); no lot-coverage limit and no density-by-lot-area limit anywhere in the chapter. Amenity 23.45.522.A.2: 5% of the gross floor area of a residential structure (private areas at least 60 sq ft and 6 ft; common 250 sq ft and 10 ft); no area for one unit added to a residential structure existing as of Jan 1 1982 (H). Landscaping 23.45.524.A.2.b: Green Factor 0.5 only for more than one new unit that increases floor area; street trees with any development. Design standards 23.45.529 apply chapter-wide. Not screened: 23.45.528 (MR structure depth on lots over 9,000 sq ft, principal structures), 23.45.520 (HR upper-level standards), 23.45.509 (area-specific provisions, for example MR (M1) University District).

**Neighborhood Commercial and Commercial 1 - Chapter 23.47A.** Use 23.47A.004 Table A row J: residential uses are P in NC1, NC2, NC3 and C1 and CU in C2 (so C2 is not covered); street-level residential limits (23.47A.005.C) and street-level development standards (23.47A.008) are stated, never assessed. Setbacks 23.47A.014: none at ground level except where a lot abuts, or is across an alley from, a residential zone (B.1 corner triangle 15 ft, B.2-B.3 upper-level setbacks above 13 ft, B.5 no opening within 5 ft of an abutting residentially zoned lot); the abutting-zone fact is read from Seattle's zoning layer within 20 ft of the lot and is REQUIRES_VERIFICATION when unread. Height 23.47A.012.A: the height mapped on the Official Land Use Map (the number in the designation), +4 ft or +7 ft under A.1; the lowest mapped height is 30 ft. FAR 23.47A.013 Tables A and B: 2.5 at 30 ft up to 8.25 at 200 ft (Station Area Overlay District figures differ), applied only as the lowest figure (2.5). Amenity 23.47A.024.A: 5% of the gross floor area in residential use, no 1982 exemption. Landscaping 23.47A.016.A.2: Green Factor 0.3 only for more than four new dwelling units. No general lot-coverage limit (limits appear only in the area-specific standards of 23.47A.009, which are not applied). Separation: none between an ADU and another structure (only provisions for structures over 250 ft wide and optional facade breaks).

**Encoding rules.** A figure from the code is encoded as a threshold only where it is unconditional; every exemption, exception, courtyard, overlay and unknown fact leads to REQUIRES_VERIFICATION and never a definite FAIL (commercial height beyond the mapped limit is always a verification item because the exceptions are not all evaluated). Rows: `adu-mrhr-*` (17, scopes MR / HR / MR,HR) and `adu-mr-separation-2026`, `adu-hr-no-separation-2026`; `adu-comm-*` (12, scope NC,C1).
